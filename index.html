Hover Soccer – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

This policy explains what information the browser game Hover Soccer ("the game", "we") collects, why, and the choices you have.

Contact: bentulka@gmail.com

1. Summary

  • We collect anonymous gameplay statistics to understand how the game is played and to improve it.
  • We do not collect your name, e-mail address, account name, contact details or any text you type.
  • You can turn analytics off at any time in the game: Settings → ANALYTICS ON/OFF, or OPT OUT on the first-launch notice.

2. What we collect

When analytics is on, the game sends gameplay events such as:

  • session start (platform, device type desktop/tablet/mobile, input type keyboard/touch, system language, graphics quality level, game version) and loading time;
  • game mode and difficulty, match start/end, score, match duration, goals and how they were scored, matches left early;
  • tutorial progress, power-ups picked up/used, cosmetic items unlocked, bought or equipped with in-game coins, achievements, daily missions and login streak, player level;
  • whether an advertisement was shown, skipped or unavailable (we do not receive any advertising data);
  • performance (frame-rate range) and technical error counts (a short code derived from the error, never the full error text).

Our analytics provider also processes, as part of normal internet communication, technical data such as your IP address, browser/device information, approximate country, and a random identifier stored in your browser (cookie / local storage) that lets it count returning players. This identifier is not linked to your name or your platform account.

3. Why we collect it (legal basis)

To fix bugs, balance difficulty and improve the game. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in improving the game (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) and, where required by law, your consent – which you can withdraw at any time with the opt-out.

4. Who processes the data

  • ByteBrew (ByteBrew, Inc., https://bytebrew.io) – analytics provider, used only in the CrazyGames version of the game. Data may be processed in the United States. See ByteBrew's privacy policy: https://docs.bytebrew.io/BBSettings/privacypolicy . CrazyGames, as an official ByteBrew partner, may be given read access to the aggregated statistics of this game.
  • CrazyGames (https://www.crazygames.com) – the game portal. CrazyGames runs its own SDK (ads, saving progress, basic statistics) and is an independent controller of that data under its own privacy policy: https://www.crazygames.com/privacy-policy . Its cookie banner covers CrazyGames' own cookies only.
  • Playgama – in the Playgama version, gameplay events are sent only through Playgama's own Bridge analytics; no third-party analytics is loaded. Playgama processes this data under its own privacy policy.

We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

5. Retention

Analytics data is kept by ByteBrew only as long as needed for game statistics, and in any case no longer than 24 months; aggregated statistics may be kept longer.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Opt out: Settings → ANALYTICS OFF (or OPT OUT on the notice). The choice is stored on your device; from then on the game sends no analytics events and stops ByteBrew tracking. Clearing your browser data resets the choice.
  • You can also block cookies/local storage or use a content blocker; the game keeps working.
  • Depending on where you live (e.g. EU/UK GDPR, California CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to processing of your data, and to complain to a data-protection authority. Because the data is anonymous to us, please include the approximate date and platform when contacting us at bentulka@gmail.com.

7. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 (16 where applicable) and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. The analytics described above does not ask for or collect personal details.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version.
